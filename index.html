01 / WHY THIS SPACE EXISTS

Not a place to
preach.
A place to explore.

I created this website as a practitioner and blogger—to record what I learn, ask better questions and share useful perspectives with others interested in inner practice.

Some things belong to personal experience. Some belong to traditional teachings. Some can be investigated scientifically. I want to keep those distinctions clear.

02 / THE PRACTICE

A structured approach
to inner practice.

YOI is described through a combination of movement, postures, breathing, chanting/sound and meditation. This site presents that framework as an independent overview.

01Movement

Prepare the body and bring attention into physical awareness.

02Postures

Yoga postures form part of the structured practice.

03Breathing

Pranayama and controlled breathing connect breath and attention.

04Sound

Chanting and sound are included within the broader practice.

05Meditation

Attention turns inward through meditative practice.
